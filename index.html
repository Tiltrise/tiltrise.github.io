How it plays

Three weights, one beam, no safety net

Blocks fall onto a beam balanced on a single ball. Light, medium and heavy pieces push it differently, so where you drop each one matters as much as how high you build.

Balance the weight

The beam tips toward the heavy side. Lean too far and the tower slides, wobbles and falls in slow motion.

Watch the sky

Wind pushes falling pieces sideways, rain makes them heavier and snow makes them slide. On the moon it's comet showers and solar wind.

Climb into the clouds

Classic gets harder the higher you go. Up in the sky, pieces fall faster and only heavy blocks are left.

Short description

Stack falling blocks on a tilting beam. Beat the wind, rain and snow. Don't tip!

About the game

Tilt Rise is a calm-but-tense balancing puzzle. Blocks fall onto a beam that pivots in the middle, and every block shifts the balance. Players place light, medium and heavy pieces with care while wind, rain and snow change how they behave, and the tower gets harder to hold up the higher it climbs. Each of the nine launch scenes reacts to the weather and hides small funny moments in the background, and seasonal events like Halloween Hollow and the Harvest Table add limited-time goals.

Features

  • Real physics: towers lean, slide and topple
  • Weather that changes the rules: wind, rain and snow
  • Classic, Easy, Medium and Hard modes, plus Zen, Tour and a Daily Tower
  • Nine scenes at launch and seasonal events
  • Power-ups (Balance, Settle and Sealer) and a daily gift
  • Share a video clip of your best collapse
