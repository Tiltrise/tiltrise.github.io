Tilt Rise Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

Tilt Rise ("the game") is made by Tilt Rise Games ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. The game is designed for players of all ages. It has no accounts, no sign-in, and no chat.

Information that stays on your device

Your settings, best scores, daily gift streak, saved power-ups and a record of recent games are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. The first time you play, the game asks for your birth year. That answer also stays on your device. It is used only to choose the right ad settings, as described below.

The game can keep a short video of the last few seconds of play so you can share your collapse. This video stays on your device. It only leaves your device if you choose to share it, through your phone's own share menu, with the app you pick.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional video ads that a player chooses to watch, for example to continue a game, double a daily gift or earn a power-up. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's ad software collects:

  • your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location (not your precise location);
  • interactions with ads and the app, such as app launches, ad views and taps;
  • diagnostic information, such as app and ad performance;
  • device identifiers, such as the advertising ID and app set ID.

This information is sent to Google over an encrypted connection. For players 13 and older, Google may use it to show personalized ads, where the law requires it only with your consent. You can change your choice with "Privacy choices" in the game menu (when shown), and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings. Google's practices are described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Players under 13

If the birth year shows a player is under 13, or no answer is given, every ad request is marked as directed to a child. Google then shows only non-personalized ads rated for general audiences and handles the request under its rules for children's apps. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the game never asks for a name, email, photo or precise location.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. The information listed above goes only to Google so the game can show ads. We do not run our own servers and do not receive or store any information about you.

Keeping and deleting information

Uninstalling the game removes everything it stored on your device. Information collected by Google's ad software is kept and deleted under Google's policies, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device's settings. If you have a question or concern, contact us at the address below.

Changes

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding purchases or game analytics, we will update this page and the effective date above.
